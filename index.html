Guitar Journey

Strings, stories, and the soundtrack of my life

Music is my escape from code

What started as a casual interest turned into a lifelong passion. The guitar taught me discipline, creativity, and the beauty of imperfection — lessons that translate directly into my tech career.

I picked up my first guitar in 2024. It was a dusty acoustic from a local shop, and my fingers hurt for weeks. But something clicked during those late-night practice sessions. Today, I play both acoustic and electric, exploring genres from blues and classic rock to modern fingerstyle.

Music keeps me balanced. After a day of debugging SQL queries and resolving client tickets, there's nothing like losing yourself in a solo or nailing a complex chord progression.
